## Image rules (Brand Identity Book §05)

- Warm, golden-hour light; doors, gateways and Madinah architecture; our own residences and team.
- Guests shown with respect, often from behind. Women only modestly dressed with a head covering. Per current direction, no photos of women or girls.
- Avoid cold/blue/grey stock, generic hotel and skyline clichés, anything at odds with halal hospitality (e.g. alcohol), and images we have no rights to use.
- Never put prices or offers over images of the Haramain.
